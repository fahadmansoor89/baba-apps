Baba Videos

A parent-controlled video player: kids watch only the videos you approve.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Baba Videos does not collect any personal information from children.

  • Your list of approved videos and categories is stored on your device.
  • Parent sign-in: a parent signs in with Google or Apple so the lists stay the same on the family's devices. We store your account's email address, a user ID, and your video list, categories and the first names you give your child profiles in Google Firebase (Sydney, Australia), visible only to your account. It is used only to sync your devices and is never sold or shared.
  • You can delete your account and its synced data at any time in the app: parent area, Settings, Account Settings, Delete Account.
  • The app has no ads of its own, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
  • Children cannot search, comment, share videos or follow links out of the app.
  • Videos play in YouTube's official embedded player. YouTube may collect data under its own terms: YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting the app removes all data from the device. If this policy changes, this page will be updated before the change is released.

Support

To open the parent area, press and hold the yellow lock (top right) for one second, then enter your PIN. There you can add videos by pasting a YouTube link, and manage your account and child profiles under Account Settings.
