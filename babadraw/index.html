Baba Draw

A simple colouring app for children aged 2 to 5.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Baba Draw does not collect, store, or share any personal information from children or adults.

  • No accounts, sign-in, names, emails, or photos are requested.
  • Drawings are saved only on your device and are never uploaded.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code.
  • The app has no internet connection, no links out of the app, and no in-app purchases.

Deleting the app removes all drawings from the device. If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated before the change is released.

Support

Tap a picture on the home screen to colour it. Pick a colour and a tool (pencil, crayon, brush or spray). Colour stays inside the lines of the shape you start in. Use the undo button to take back a mark, or the bin to start the page again. Tap the small coloured picture to see an example.
